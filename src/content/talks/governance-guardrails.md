title: "Governance that doesn't slow anyone down: guardrails for Power BI at scale"
abstract: "Governance usually arrives as a set of rules nobody can operate, and the estate routes around it within a quarter. This session covers the guardrails that hold: domains and workspaces drawn on real boundaries, app-based distribution instead of direct links, and access assigned to groups rather than to people. The test is simple: a control that needs a meeting will not survive."
order: 2
