title: "Why your semantic model is slow — and the four things that actually fix it"
abstract: "Slow semantic models are usually blamed on data volume, and usually should not be. This session walks the four causes that account for most of what I find in audits: cardinality in the wrong column, calculated columns that should be measures, relationship direction, and a storage mode chosen by habit. Each one with how to confirm it before changing anything."
