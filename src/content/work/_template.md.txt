## Context

The organization, the users, the volume. Anonymized on the agreed pattern: "a Japanese
industrial manufacturer, Americas division", "a fuel and energy distribution group". Numbers
rounded, and framed as ratios wherever the absolute figure is sensitive.

## Constraint

What made it hard. Capacity limits, source system shape, refresh window, a governance rule,
an SLA. Be specific: "capacity F2 does not sustain incremental refresh above ~50M rows"
beats "performance could degrade".

## Decision

What you chose, **and what you rejected and why**. This section is why anyone reads the page.

## Result

Measurable. Refresh time, model size, adoption, tickets avoided.

## What I'd do differently

One honest paragraph. This is the credibility multiplier and almost nobody writes it.
