# Speaking slide templates

Authored at 1920×1080 and scaled with `Fit` for preview. Four types:

| Type | Ground | Opener | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TitleSlide` | paper | 14px full-bleed rule | full wordmark at 52px + role line |
| `DividerSlide` | inverted | numbered part label | 6px accent rule under the title |
| `ContentSlide` | paper | 2px accent tick + kicker | three columns on 2px rules; body never below 28px |
| `ClosingSlide` | inverted | — | contact grid on hairlines |

Every slide carries the corner mark (handle + monogram) bottom-right — the monogram is used here because the corner is under 96px of usable width.
