One-line: the wordmark plus a role line, divided by a hairline — the header and signature form of the mark.

```jsx
<Lockup role="Head of Operations — Data & AI" size={24} />
<Lockup align="column" tone="inverse" />
```

The role line is the ONLY text ever locked to the mark, and it is a role, never a tagline or a promise. Below 360px of available width, switch `align` to column.
